The Pride of Western Odisha

Sambalpur is one of the most culturally rich cities in Odisha. Known for its colorful Sambalpuri sarees, folk dances, music traditions, and welcoming people, the city beautifully preserves its heritage while embracing modern growth.

The city is home to the world-famous Hirakud Dam built across the mighty Mahanadi River. It attracts thousands of tourists every year with its scenic beauty, peaceful reservoirs, and breathtaking sunset views.

Sambalpur also holds deep spiritual significance through the revered Maa Samaleswari Temple, one of the most important pilgrimage destinations in Odisha.

Culture

Sambalpur is famous for Sambalpuri dance, folk music, handloom textiles, and vibrant festivals like Nuakhai celebrated with great enthusiasm.

Tourism

Major attractions include Hirakud Dam, Samaleswari Temple, Debrigarh Wildlife Sanctuary, and beautiful river landscapes.

Food

Traditional Odia cuisine, sweets, street food, and local delicacies make Sambalpur a delightful destination for food lovers.
