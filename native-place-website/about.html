About Sambalpur

Sambalpur is one of the most beautiful and culturally rich cities in Western Odisha. The city is famous for temples, education, tourism, handloom culture, and the mighty Hirakud Dam.

Maa Samaleswari Temple

Maa Samaleswari Temple is the most important temple in Sambalpur and one of the major spiritual destinations of Odisha. The temple is dedicated to Goddess Samaleswari, who is worshipped as the presiding deity of Western Odisha.

Thousands of devotees visit the temple every year, especially during festivals and Navratri celebrations. The temple reflects the spiritual identity and faith of Sambalpur people.

Ghanteswari Temple

Ghanteswari Temple is located near the banks of the Mahanadi River and is famous for thousands of bells offered by devotees.

The temple is surrounded by peaceful natural beauty and attracts tourists for its calm atmosphere, river views, and religious importance.

Hirakud Dam & Burla

Hirakud Dam is one of the longest earthen dams in the world, built across the mighty Mahanadi River. It is one of the biggest attractions of Sambalpur.

Burla town near Hirakud is famous for engineering, medical education, and beautiful scenic landscapes. The dam offers stunning sunset views and attracts tourists throughout the year.

Sambalpur University

Sambalpur University is one of the leading universities of Odisha. It provides higher education in arts, science, commerce, law, and research.

The university campus is surrounded by greenery and creates a peaceful environment for students. It plays a major role in educational development of Western Odisha.

SUIIT

Sambalpur University Institute of Information Technology (SUIIT) is one of the top engineering institutions in Western Odisha.

It is known for quality education in Computer Science, Electronics, and Engineering fields. Students from different parts of Odisha come here for technical education.

GM University

GM University is a well-known educational institution in Sambalpur offering courses in science, commerce, management, and technology.

The university is focused on skill development, innovation, and modern education systems.

VIMSAR Medical College

Veer Surendra Sai Institute of Medical Sciences and Research (VIMSAR) is one of Odisha’s top government medical colleges.

Located in Burla, VIMSAR provides medical education, healthcare services, and advanced treatment facilities to thousands of people.

Zero Point

Zero Point is one of the most scenic tourist spots near Hirakud Dam. It offers panoramic views of water reservoirs, mountains, and forests.

The location is perfect for photography, sightseeing, and enjoying peaceful natural beauty.

Cypone

Cypone area in Sambalpur is known for greenery, peaceful roads, and nearby natural attractions.

The place is becoming popular among visitors because of its calm environment and beautiful surroundings.
